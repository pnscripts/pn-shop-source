# Upgrade notes: 1.3.0

Pricing and business customers: price lists per customer group, quantity tiers, net prices, minimum orders, payment by invoice and prices for signed-in customers only. Plugin and theme contracts stay compatible: product cards and pages receive new props, and existing props keep their meaning.

```bash
composer update pnscripts/pn-shop-core --with-all-dependencies
php artisan pnshop:update
php artisan optimize && php artisan queue:restart
```

Three migrations:

- `price_lists` and `price_list_entries`;
- `customer_groups.min_order_total` and `customer_groups.prices_include_tax`;
- `payment_methods.customer_group_ids`.

Nothing changes for a shop until it creates a price list or uses the new options.

## Required actions

- **Staff permissions:** the new permission `catalog.prices.manage` (*Manage price lists*) is granted to administrators. Give it to other roles that should manage prices.
- **Rebuild themes:** non-default themes, Aurora included, must be rebuilt and republished to show quantity tiers, "excl. tax" and "Sign in to see prices":

  ```bash
  npm run build:theme -- <vendor/name>
  php artisan pnshop:theme publish <vendor/name>
  ```

  Until then they keep working as before. Rebuild them **before** turning off *Show prices to guests*: an older product page expects a price and fails when it is hidden.

## New

- **Price lists** (Admin → Catalog → Price lists): prices for a customer group or for everyone, with optional dates, quantity tiers, and CSV import and export. Admin API: `/price-lists` and `/price-lists/{id}/prices`.
- **Customer groups:** show prices with or without tax, and a minimum order.
- **Payment methods:** can be limited to customer groups.
- **Invoice (pay later)** gateway with payment terms.
- **Setting** *Customers → Show prices to guests*.

See [Pricing and business customers](../ecommerce/pricing.md).

## Behaviour changes

- **One price everywhere:** product cards, product pages, the cart, checkout, orders, invoices, structured data and the Store API all take prices from the new `catalog.price` pipeline. Without price lists the prices are the same as before: the sale price when there is one.
- **Order lines:** `sale_price` on an order line holds what the customer paid per unit whenever it is below the price, including a price-list or tier price.
- **Unpaid orders:** orders paid by invoice are never cancelled by *Cancel unpaid orders after*.

## For theme and plugin authors

These props are new and optional; older themes ignore them:

- Product cards: `price_includes_tax`; `price` and `sale_price` are `null` when prices are hidden from guests.
- `shop/show`:
  - `product.price_includes_tax` and `product.prices_visible`;
  - `product.variants[].tiers` (list of `{min_quantity, price}`);
  - `variants[].price` may be `null`.
- Cart: `cart.minimum_order` (money or `null`).

Plugins can add stages to the `catalog.price` pipeline: see [Pricing → For plugin authors](../ecommerce/pricing.md#for-plugin-authors). `ProductVariant::unitPrice($quantity)` and `isOnSale($quantity)` return the current customer's price.
